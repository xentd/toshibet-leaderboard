Fair play

Rules

Who can take part

You must be 18 or older, or the legal age where you live, and ToshiBet must be available in your country. Check your local laws before you play. Only accounts registered through the OFFICIAL link are tracked.

How ranking works

Rankings use total wagered on eligible games during the race period. Totals update periodically, so the board can lag behind live play. The time of the last update is shown on the board.

One account per person

Multiple accounts, account sharing and any abuse of the leaderboard lead to disqualification and forfeited prizes.

Prizes and verification

Winners are verified before any prize is paid. Staff decisions on rankings and prizes are final.
